Solution tracks

Five specialist programmes, each triggered by a diagnostic finding.

Tracks are never proposed on assumption. They are scoped after the diagnostic, can run in parallel, and end with tools your team operates independently.

Triggered by demand forecast accuracy gaps or no formal S&OP process

S&OP deployment & demand planning

We design and deploy a formal Sales & Operations Planning cycle and, where needed, a statistical demand model calibrated to your categories and Egyptian market seasonality. The cycle is handed over running.

  • S&OP process design: cadence, templates, RACI, governance
  • Three facilitated live cycles with handover to an internal owner
  • Statistical demand model (baseline, seasonality, promotional uplift)
  • Demand planning dashboard: forecast accuracy, bias, exceptions
Triggered by overstock, stockouts or no systematic replenishment parameters

Inventory optimisation & replenishment

Fix the cash-in-stock problem with inventory intelligence, scientific safety stocks and a replenishment engine that replaces gut-feel ordering.

  • Inventory visibility dashboard
  • Safety stock and reorder point optimisation
  • Replenishment engine (Excel, Power BI or hosted)
  • Capacity planning tool
  • Slow and dead stock action plan
Triggered by customer OTIF below 90%, supplier unreliability or stockout-driven lost sales

Service level & control tower

Improve customer service through supplier improvement programmes and an end-to-end control tower that shows where orders are at risk before the customer notices.

  • Supplier improvement programme
  • End-to-end control tower dashboard
  • Service level root cause analysis
Triggered by logistics cost above benchmark, margin erosion or unquantified operational losses

Loss elimination & cost project execution

A shelf-back loss elimination workshop surfaces hidden cost projects; we then score, prioritise and lead the ones with the best return.

  • Loss elimination workshop
  • Cost-to-serve analysis
  • Project scoring and prioritisation
  • Project implementation (PM lead)
  • Savings tracking dashboard
Triggered by team capability gaps, undocumented processes or improvement regression risk

Training & SOP development

Upskill the supply chain team and document every key process so that improvements are locked in after the engagement ends.

  • Supply chain fundamentals and advanced demand planning training
  • Custom training programmes
  • Three-month coaching programme
  • Eight-section SOPs: process flow, RACI, controls, exception handling

Our approach

From insight to impact.

A structured, evidence-driven path from diagnosis to implementation. Every engagement starts with a diagnostic — no assumptions, no pre-packaged solutions.

Methodology

Five steps, one discipline.

The diagnostic findings determine which solutions are proposed, so every recommendation is grounded in your actual data and context.

  1. Understand

    We deep-dive into your business and challenges through workshops, interviews and process mapping.

  2. Diagnose

    We analyse your data across cost, cash, service and digital, and identify root causes and quantified opportunities.

  3. Design

    We build scenarios and design the optimal solution — with a financial return estimate before you commit.

  4. Implement

    We execute with you: tools built, processes deployed, knowledge transferred.

  5. Sustain

    We track impact through savings dashboards and continuous improvement support.

The diagnostic framework

Four pillars. No pillar is skipped.

Every diagnostic assesses performance across all four dimensions, so the result is a complete picture rather than a partial view.

Cost

  • Cost-to-serve by customer / channel / SKU
  • Logistics cost mapping by route
  • Procurement spend analysis
  • Waste and loss tree (shelf-back)
  • Cost reduction opportunity sizing

Cash

  • Inventory value and aging by location
  • Working capital cycle (DIO, DSO, DPO)
  • Days of cover vs. optimal by category
  • Slow and dead stock quantification
  • Cash release opportunity

Service

  • Customer OTIF measurement
  • Stockout frequency and revenue impact
  • Supplier reliability scorecard
  • Order fulfilment lead time
  • Returns and quality rejection rate

Digital

  • Systems and tools landscape
  • Data quality and availability
  • Planning process and tools review
  • Team capability scoring (1–5)
  • Automation opportunity mapping
